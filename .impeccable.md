# StackNest — Design Context

**Product**: A Chrome new-tab replacement / tab & bookmark manager. Stash open
windows into collections, organize into Spaces, tag links, find & remove
duplicates, notes & to-dos with reminders, My Space + a PIN Vault, unified
search, glanceable market ticker.

**Target audience**: Power users and knowledge workers with heavy tab/bookmark
load who want to declutter and organize their browser. They open this dozens of
times a day — it must feel calm, fast, and out of the way. ADHD-friendly:
unmistakable "you are here", nothing important hover-only, one accent.

**Use cases**: Stash a window's tabs in one click; browse/organize collections
on a board (columns / tiles / mosaic); tag & cross-reference links; hunt down
duplicates; quick keyboard-first search; check crypto/FX at a glance.

**Brand personality / tone — "Precision" (2026-10-06):**
- An instrument panel, not a dashboard. Craft & precision, Swiss-technical.
- References: a framed bento card with a lava block and hatched header; the
  Dark Void / Liquid Lava / Gluon / Slate / Dusty / Snow palette; an orange
  wall behind frosted glass; a monospace technical poster with orange notes.
- A Dark Void frame with rounded paper panels set into it. The sidebar is on
  the frame; the work is on the panels.
- One accent: Liquid Lava (#F56E0F; #E8590C on light paper). State only — plus
  the full stop that ends every view title and the wordmark. User data keeps
  its own colours as wayfinding. Danger is crimson, far from the lava.
- Rectangles hold, pills act, single glyphs are circles.
- Mono annotates (lowercase captions with hairline rules, the lava kicker,
  counts, domains, dates); large light numerals for counts that matter.
- Hatching only on space waiting to be filled.

**Themes**: Light (ink on paper, void frame) · Dark (snow on void, deeper
void frame) · Linen (all paper, walnut ink, pine accent).

**Type**: Hanken Grotesk (UI, headings) + JetBrains Mono (captions, counts,
data). Scale `--t1..--t7` = 10.5 · 11.5 · 13 · 14.5 · 16.5 · 18.5 · 24px,
each multiplied by a per-role dial (Settings › Appearance › Text sizes).

**Spacing scale** (`--s1..--s9`): 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 40 · 48.
**Radius scale** (`--r1..--r6`): 4 · 6 · 8 · 10 · 12 · 16; `--panel-r` 18;
`--pill` for controls.
