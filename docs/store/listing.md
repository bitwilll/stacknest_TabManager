## Detailed description

Plain text. The store does not render Markdown, so paste everything between the rules as it is.

---

StackNest turns every new tab into a quiet workspace for the tabs you have open, the ones you want to keep, and your bookmarks.

Save a whole window as a collection and close it, then bring it back when you need it. Browse and tidy your Chrome bookmarks. Clear out links you saved twice. Keep notes, checklists and reminders beside the work they belong to. Everything stays in your browser.

WHAT YOU GET
• Live tabs: this window's tabs at a glance. Switch, close, or drag one into a collection.
• Collections: save or stash a window in one click, group collections into spaces (say, Personal and Work), reopen a set in a new window, export to a standard bookmarks file.
• Three board layouts: columns, tiles or mosaic.
• Library: your Chrome bookmarks, folder by folder. Create, rename, move, delete, open all.
• Duplicates: finds links saved more than once across your collections and bookmarks, and keeps the copy you choose.
• Tags: tag any saved link or bookmark, then browse by tag or as a mind-graph.
• My Space and Vault: move bookmarks out of Chrome and into StackNest, and keep some behind a PIN. The PIN is a privacy screen, not encryption.
• Notes and to-dos: notes with Markdown, checklists, and reminders that arrive as browser notifications.
• Search: one field filters tabs, collections and bookmarks. Press ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows) or / to jump to it.
• Undo: deleted something by mistake? Undo puts it back.
• Your look: light, dark and Linen themes, a sidebar that folds to an icon rail, and adjustable interface and text sizes.
• Backup: export everything to a file, or back it up to a private app folder in your own Google Drive.
• Market ticker: optional crypto and currency prices beside the search bar. Off by default.

PRIVACY
StackNest has no account, no analytics and no ads. Your data is stored by Chrome on your computer, and nothing is sent to the developer. Google Drive backup and the market ticker are the only features that use the network, and both stay off until you turn them on.
